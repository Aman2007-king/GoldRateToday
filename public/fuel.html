🛢️ How are fuel prices fixed?

Fuel prices in India are revised daily at 6 AM by oil marketing companies (IOCL, BPCL, HPCL) based on international crude oil prices, USD/INR exchange rate, and central/state taxes & duties.

📊 Why do prices differ by city?

State VAT on petrol and diesel varies significantly — from ~16% in Goa to ~35%+ in Maharashtra and Rajasthan. This is the primary reason for the large city-to-city variation in fuel prices.
