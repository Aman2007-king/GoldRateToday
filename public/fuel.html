🛢️ How Are Fuel Prices Fixed?

Indian OMCs (IOCL, BPCL, HPCL) revise prices daily at 6 AM based on international crude oil, USD/INR exchange rate, refinery throughput costs, and central+state taxes & duties. Since June 2017, prices change daily via Dynamic Fuel Pricing.

📊 Why Prices Differ by City?

State VAT is the primary differentiator — from ~16% in Goa to ~38% in Maharashtra and Rajasthan. Additional local levies and transport distance also play a role. Central excise duty is uniform across India at ₹19.90/L for petrol.

🌿 What About CNG?

CNG prices are set by City Gas Distribution (CGD) companies and regulated by PNGRB. CNG is typically 40–50% cheaper than petrol on a per-km cost basis for vehicles that run on it. Available primarily in metro and Tier-1 cities.

🥇 Crude Oil & Gold Correlation

When crude oil rises, transport and production costs increase — often pushing gold and food prices higher. A weaker rupee (USD/INR rising) increases both crude import cost and gold import cost simultaneously for India.
