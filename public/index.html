📌 What Drives Gold Prices in India?

Gold prices depend on international USD rates (LBMA), the USD/INR exchange rate, and India's import duty (~15%). MCX gold prices are updated during trading hours 9 AM–11:30 PM.

🔖 Why Do Rates Differ by City?

Local taxes, MMTC levies, and transport costs create city-level premiums. Chennai typically has the highest rates; Kolkata often has the lowest due to proximity to refining centres.

💎 What is BIS Hallmark & HUID?

BIS Hallmark guarantees purity. Since June 2021, a 6-digit HUID is mandatory on all gold jewellery sold in India. Verify via the BIS Care App before purchasing.

📈 Sovereign Gold Bonds vs Physical Gold

SGBs issued by RBI offer 2.5% annual interest and are exempt from capital gains tax at maturity (8 years). Best financial gold investment without storage risk.

What is the difference between 22K and 24K gold?+
24K gold is 99.9% pure. 22K is 91.6% pure (916 hallmark), alloyed with copper/silver for durability. Most Indian jewellery is 22K. 18K (75%) is used in designer jewellery.
Is GST applicable on gold purchases?+
Yes. GST on gold is 3% on the gold value + 5% on making charges. SGBs have no GST and no capital gains tax at maturity, making them tax-efficient.
