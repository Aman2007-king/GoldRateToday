📌 What Drives Gold Prices in India?

Gold prices depend on international USD rates (LBMA), the USD/INR exchange rate, and India's import duty (~15%). MCX gold prices are updated during trading hours from 9:00 AM to 11:30 PM.

🔖 Why Do Rates Differ by City?

Local taxes, MMTC levies, and transport costs create city-level premiums. Chennai typically has the highest rates; Kolkata often has the lowest due to proximity to refining centres.

💎 What is BIS Hallmark & HUID?

BIS Hallmark guarantees purity. From June 2021, a 6-digit HUID (Hallmark Unique ID) is mandatory on all gold jewellery. Verify via the BIS Care App before purchasing.

📈 Sovereign Gold Bonds vs Physical Gold

SGBs issued by RBI offer 2.5% annual interest and are exempt from capital gains tax at maturity (8 years). They're the best financial tool for gold investment without storage risk.

What is the difference between 22K and 24K gold?+
24K gold is 99.9% pure and too soft for jewellery. 22K gold is 91.6% pure (916 hallmark), alloyed with copper or silver for durability. Most Indian jewellery is 22K. 18K (75% gold) is used in designer jewellery.
Is GST applicable on gold purchases?+
Yes. GST on gold is 3% on the gold value + 5% on making charges. On SGBs, there's no GST at purchase and no capital gains tax at maturity, making them far more tax-efficient than physical gold.
When is the best time to buy gold in India?+
Historically, gold prices dip slightly between March–June before the wedding season. Akshaya Tritiya and Dhanteras are auspicious buying days. However, from a purely financial standpoint, SGB windows offer the best value.
How are gold rates updated on this website?+
Our server automatically fetches gold and silver rates from multiple APIs (including metals-api.com and goldprice.org) every 5 minutes. The backend computes city-wise rates by applying local premium adjustments to the Mumbai base rate.
