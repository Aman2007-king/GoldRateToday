📌 What drives gold prices?

Gold prices in India follow LBMA USD benchmark × USD/INR exchange rate + India's ~15% import duty. MCX gold updates live during 9 AM–11:30 PM trading window.

🏙️ Why do rates differ by city?

Local state taxes, MMTC logistics, and local demand cause city-level differences. Chennai rates are typically highest; Kolkata and Patna among the lowest.

🔖 What is BIS Hallmark & HUID?

Since June 2021, a 6-digit HUID is mandatory on all gold jewellery in India. Verify any HUID on the official BIS Care App before purchasing to confirm purity.

📈 Sovereign Gold Bond (SGB)

SGBs are RBI-issued bonds denominated in grams of gold. They pay 2.5% annual interest and are fully exempt from capital gains tax on maturity (8 years).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is today's gold rate in India? +
Today's 24K gold rate is loading…. Rates are updated twice daily (10 AM & 6 PM IST) from live international benchmarks. City rates may vary slightly due to local taxes.
What is the difference between 22K and 24K gold? +
24K gold is 99.9% pure and too soft for jewellery. 22K (hallmarked 916) is 91.6% pure — alloyed with copper or silver for durability. Most Indian jewellery is 22K.
How much GST is charged on gold? +
GST on gold bullion is 3%. On jewellery, it is 3% on the gold value + 5% on making charges. Always ask for an itemised bill showing gold rate, making charge, and GST separately.
What is 1 tola of gold in grams? +
1 Tola = 11.6638 grams. It is a traditional Indian unit still widely used in gold markets, especially in North India. Our calculator supports tola input directly.
When is the best time to buy gold in India? +
Historically, gold prices soften slightly in March–June. For investment purposes, Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGB) offered by RBI are the most cost-efficient as they have zero making charges and earn 2.5% annual interest.

About Gold Rates in India

Gold rates in India are determined by the Multi Commodity Exchange (MCX), the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS), and international spot prices from the London Bullion Market Association (LBMA). The price you see at a jeweller includes the base metal price, making charges (5–25%), and 3% GST on the gold value.

India is the world's second-largest consumer of gold, buying approximately 700–800 tonnes annually. Around 60% goes toward jewellery and 40% toward investment (coins, bars, Sovereign Gold Bonds, digital gold). Southern India — especially Kerala and Tamil Nadu — accounts for roughly 40% of all national gold consumption.

Before purchasing jewellery, always check for the BIS hallmark (BIS logo + purity grade like 22K916 + HUID). You can verify any HUID on the BIS Care App to confirm the jeweller's licence and the gold's certified purity level.
