📌 What Drives Gold Prices in India?

Gold prices depend on international USD rates (LBMA), USD/INR exchange rate, and India's import duty (currently ~15%). MCX gold prices are updated in real-time during trading hours.

🔖 Why Do Rates Differ by City?

Local taxes, MMTC levies, and transport costs create city-level premiums. Chennai typically has the highest rates; Kolkata often has the lowest due to proximity to refining centres.

💎 What is BIS Hallmark & HUID?

BIS Hallmark guarantees purity. From June 2021, a 6-digit HUID (Hallmark Unique ID) is mandatory on all gold jewellery. Always verify via the BIS Care App before purchasing.

📈 Should You Buy Gold Now?

Gold is typically bought as a hedge against inflation and rupee depreciation. Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGBs) offered by RBI provide 2.5% annual interest with no GST on maturity.

What is the difference between 22K and 24K gold?
24K gold is 99.9% pure and too soft for jewellery. 22K gold is 91.6% pure, alloyed with copper or silver for durability. Most Indian jewellery is 22K (hallmarked as 916).
Is GST applicable on gold purchases?
Yes. GST on gold jewellery is 3% on the gold price + 5% on making charges. On total jewellery purchases, most buyers pay roughly 3-4% extra in taxes.
What is Sovereign Gold Bond (SGB)?
SGBs are RBI-issued bonds denominated in grams of gold. They pay 2.5% annual interest and are exempt from capital gains tax if held to maturity (8 years). They are the best way to invest in gold without physical holding.
When is the best time to buy gold in India?
Historically, gold prices dip slightly between March–June. Akshaya Tritiya and Dhanteras are culturally auspicious days for gold buying. However, SGB windows offer the best financial value.
