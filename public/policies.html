IPO & Grey Market Framework

The IPO (Initial Public Offering) section of our website is one of our most visited modules. To maintain integrity, we follow these strict guidelines:

GMP (Grey Market Premium) Accuracy

GMP is an unofficial, unregulated sentiment indicator. We source GMP data from multiple "Kostak" and "Subject to Allotment" dealers across India. However, GMP should NEVER be the sole reason for an investment. It is a speculative figure that can drop to zero instantly.

Review Methodology

Our IPO "Ratings" (e.g., Subscribe, Avoid, Neutral) are based on quantitative metrics: PE Ratio vs Industry Peers, Debt-to-Equity, Revenue CAGR, and Grey Market demand. These represent our editorial opinion and are not a guarantee of profit.

Financial Glossary (A-Z)

To help our users understand the language of wealth, we have compiled this 50-term master list.

AMC: Asset Management Company. The firm that manages mutual funds.
Alpha: The excess return of an investment relative to a benchmark.
Bullion: Physical gold or silver of high purity, often in bars or coins.
Beta: A measure of a stock's volatility in relation to the overall market.
CAGR: Compound Annual Growth Rate. The mean annual growth rate of an investment over time.
Carat: A measure of the purity of gold (24K is 99.9% pure).
Dividend: A portion of company earnings paid out to shareholders.
Expense Ratio: The annual fee that all mutual funds or ETFs charge their shareholders.
Face Value: The nominal value of a security stated by the issuer.
GMP: Grey Market Premium. The premium at which IPO shares are traded informally.
HUID: Hallmark Unique Identification. A 6-digit alphanumeric code for gold.
IPO: Initial Public Offering. When a private company first sells shares to the public.
Large Cap: Companies with a large market capitalization (usually top 100).
Lumpsum: A single, one-time investment in a mutual fund.
MCX: Multi Commodity Exchange of India. Where gold/silver are traded.
NAV: Net Asset Value. The per-share value of a mutual fund scheme.
Nifty 50: The benchmark index of the National Stock Exchange of India.
PE Ratio: Price-to-Earnings ratio. A tool for valuing companies.
RHP: Red Herring Prospectus. The legal document filed before an IPO.
SIP: Systematic Investment Plan. Investing a fixed amount regularly.
Volatility: The rate at which the price of a security increases or decreases.
Yield: The earnings generated and realized on an investment over a period.
