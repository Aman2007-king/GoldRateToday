📋 What is SIP?

Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) invests a fixed amount monthly in mutual funds. It benefits from rupee cost averaging and compounding. Start with as low as ₹500/month on any SEBI-regulated fund.

🔒 Are Mutual Funds Safe?

All funds are regulated by SEBI. Your units are held by a custodian independent of the AMC — even if an AMC closes, your investment is safe. Equity funds carry market risk; liquid funds are near-risk-free.

🥇 Gold vs Equity SIP

Gold has returned ~18% in 2026, outperforming many large-cap funds. Experts recommend 10–15% allocation to gold (via Gold ETF or Sovereign Gold Bonds) as a hedge alongside equity SIPs.

💡 ELSS for Tax Saving

ELSS (Equity Linked Savings Schemes) offer Section 80C deductions up to ₹1.5L per year with only a 3-year lock-in — the shortest among 80C options. Returns are generally 12–18% historically.
