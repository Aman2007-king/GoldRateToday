📋 What is SIP?

Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) lets you invest a fixed amount in mutual funds every month. It averages your cost over time (rupee cost averaging) and benefits from compounding. Start with as low as ₹500/month.

🔒 SEBI-registered & safe?

All mutual funds in India are regulated by SEBI (Securities and Exchange Board of India). Your investment is held by a custodian, not the AMC — so even if the AMC shuts down, your money is safe.
