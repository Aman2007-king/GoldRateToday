📋 How to Apply for an IPO

Apply via ASBA in your demat account. Funds are blocked, not debited until allotment. Angel One & Zerodha support UPI IPO applications in under 2 minutes.

💡 What is GMP?

Grey Market Premium (GMP) is the unofficial premium at which shares trade before listing. It indicates demand but is speculative. Always check fundamentals — P/E, revenue, promoter stake — before applying.

⚠️ IPO Risks

IPOs can list below issue price. Read the Red Herring Prospectus (RHP). Never invest based on GMP alone. Check use of proceeds and debt-to-equity ratio.
