How to Apply for an IPO in India (2026 Guide)

Applying for an IPO has become 100% digital in 2026. You no longer need to submit physical forms. Here is the step-by-step process used by 99% of retail investors.

Step 1: Have a Demat Account
You must have a Demat and Trading account with a SEBI-registered broker (like Zerodha, Groww, or Upstox). Ensure your KYC is updated.
Step 2: Choose the IPO
Login to your broker app, go to the 'IPO' section, and select an 'Open' issue. Check the Price Band and Lot Size.
Step 3: Place Your Bid
Select the number of lots. Always tick 'Cut-off Price' to ensure your bid is valid at the final discovered price.
Step 4: UPI Mandate
Enter your UPI ID. You will receive a notification in your UPI app (GPay, PhonePe). Approve the mandate to block the funds.

Pro Tip: Only one application is allowed per PAN card. If you apply from multiple demat accounts using the same PAN, all your applications will be rejected.
