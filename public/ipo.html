📋 How to apply for an IPO?

Apply via your demat account through ASBA (Application Supported by Blocked Amount). Funds are blocked in your bank, not debited until allotment. Use Angel One, Zerodha, or Groww — all support UPI-based IPO applications.

💡 What is IPO GMP?

Grey Market Premium (GMP) is the unofficial premium at which IPO shares trade before listing. A high GMP suggests strong demand but is speculative and not guaranteed. Always assess fundamentals before investing.

📅 IPO allotment & listing timeline

Typically: Day 1-3: Subscription open → Day 4: Allotment → Day 6: Listing on NSE/BSE. Refunds for non-allotted applications are credited within 2 working days of allotment.

⚠️ IPO investing risks

IPOs can list below issue price. Check company fundamentals, P/E ratio versus peers, promoter background, and use of proceeds before applying. Do not apply based on GMP alone.
