📅 IPO Process Timeline

Subscription Open
Apply via ASBA through your demat account. Funds blocked, not debited.
Subscription Close (Day 3)
Last day to apply. UPI mandate must be approved by 5 PM.
Basis of Allotment (Day 4)
Allotment determined by NSE/BSE through a lottery for oversubscribed issues.
Refund Initiation (Day 5)
Unblocked for non-allotted applicants. Usually instant via UPI.
Listing Day (Day 6)
Shares credited to demat. Trading begins at 10 AM on NSE and BSE.

📋 How to Apply for an IPO

Apply via ASBA in your demat account. Funds are blocked, not debited until allotment. Angel One, Zerodha, and Groww all support UPI IPO applications — apply in under 2 minutes.

💡 What is IPO GMP?

Grey Market Premium (GMP) is the unofficial premium at which IPO shares trade before listing. A high GMP suggests demand but is speculative. Always analyse fundamentals — revenue, P/E, promoter stake — before applying.

⚠️ IPO Investment Risks

IPOs can list below issue price. Check the Red Herring Prospectus (RHP) for use of proceeds, promoter background, and financial ratios. Never invest based on GMP alone.
